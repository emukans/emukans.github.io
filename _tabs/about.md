I build LLM agents and document processing systems that run in production.

As ML Architect at [Graip.AI](https://graip.ai), I architect the full stack behind these systems: data pipelines, analytics and predictive models, and the infrastructure for training, serving and monitoring models at scale.

My work is grounded in research. My doctoral studies in NLP at the University of Latvia, supervised by Prof. Guntis Bārzdiņš, focused on enriching model inputs with LLM-generated context, the same idea that now powers retrieval in Agent Labs. Now I study when a task truly needs a large model and when a smaller one will do.

On this blog I write about production LLM agents, RAG, document AI and NLP research.

## Work with me

I consult on LLM agents, document AI, data engineering, data science and ML service architecture. What you get:

- **Agents you can trust in production**: orchestration, tool integrations and human approval designed so agents can act in your systems safely.
- **Better answers from your documents**: retrieval tuned with hybrid search, re-ranking and contextual enrichment.
- **Less manual data entry**: extraction pipelines that turn invoices, contracts and forms into structured data.
- **Lower model costs**: find where a smaller fine-tuned model can replace large-model calls without losing quality.
- **Confidence before release**: evaluation sets and regression checks that show whether a change actually helps.
- **A faster path from data to production**: end-to-end ML workflows and service architecture for training, serving and monitoring.
- **Data you can build on**: data pipelines and analysis that turn raw data into models and business decisions.
